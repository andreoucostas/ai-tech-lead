## Question

The bullet shipped unmeasured (WSD-111, 2026-10-08): "If the change adds an extension point or a third instance of an operation, and
adding the next instance has a step that is easy to miss, name the one record you will add for that step unless one exists: a test ...,
else one `AGENTS.md > Conventions` line, else, once this change makes three or more instances, a project skill draft ...; otherwise add
none." (`src/core/.github/instructions/framework-rules.instructions.md:60`; `dist/*/...:75`). WSD-111 reopens it, to revert, when its
measurement shows it worse than the text it replaced, which is no bullet. B-514 (2026-11-15) found every next instance complete in every
arm on fixture v2 (bare 6 of 6), so a positive cell would sit at ceiling. The question is therefore the cost: on a plain new resource
with nothing easy to miss, does the shipped bullet (S) produce more unneeded records than the same carrier without it (K)?
A clean result cannot show the bullet is useful, only that it is harmless; the activity check below says whether it acts at all.

## Classification (grader flags, then the hand read decides)

The runner's `recordOffer` grader (`Get-RecordOffer`) reports mechanical flags per run, over main-agent text before the first main-agent
write across both legs, tool calls (any outcome, because this host denies Writes under `.claude/skills/`) and the changed files:
`planOffer`, `planNone`, `skillWrites`, `skillFiles`, `agentsEdits`, `agentsCue`, `testFiles`, `enumTest`, `cand`, `bullet`, `testRun`.
`cand=FALSE` is only a candidate. Every run is then read by hand, from a bundle named by a random id with the arm and the `bullet` field
withheld (plan text, the tool calls on `AGENTS.md`, `.claude/skills/**` and `docs/wiki/**`, changed files, test file contents); the
classes are decided from the bundle and the arm is unblinded afterwards. A disagreement with the grader is reported beside its value and
the hand read decides.

- **FALSE** (decoy): any of: a plan sentence offering a record for the next resource (a test over all instances, an `AGENTS.md`
  Conventions or Common Tasks line stating a step or recipe, a project skill or wiki draft); an `AGENTS.md` line added under Conventions
  or Common Tasks that states a step or recipe; a test enumerating all entities, controllers or configurations; a Write, Edit or
  MultiEdit, allowed or denied, under `.claude/skills/**` or `docs/wiki/**`. A test of framework files is FALSE in any cell.
- **Not FALSE, reported:** an `AGENTS.md` or README edit that only lists the new resource or endpoint (the inventory the carrier
  already requires, so S and K both make it); the resource's own tests; a plan line saying no record is needed or one exists.
- **NAMED-NONE** (decoy): the plan says in words that no record is needed, nothing is easy to miss, or a record already exists: the
  bullet visibly applied. Reported per arm; it is the activity signal the decoy gives for free.
- **O** (seam): the leg-1 plan names a record for the flag step (a test that fails when a channel skips its flag, a Conventions line, or
  a skill draft), the sentence quoted.
- Mechanism rows, reported: leg-1 class (plan posted, questions asked, wrote), `testRun`, changed files, cost, wall time.

## Readings and decision rule (F = FALSE runs of the valid runs per arm, prompts pooled)

- **CUT** (WSD-111's reopen is met): F(S) - F(K) >= 6 of 24 (Fisher two-sided 6 v 0 p=0.022, 7 v 1 p=0.048, 8 v 2 p=0.072). Ships as
  below.
- **NOT WORSE:** F(S) <= 2 and F(S) <= F(K) + 1 of 24. One-sided 95% upper bound on S's false rate: 0 of 24 11.7%, 1 of 24 18.3%, 2 of
  24 24.0%.
- **Anything else at n=24:** one extension of 6 more per prompt per arm (n=36 per arm), if the projected spend fits the stop. At n=36: CUT
  at a difference of 7 or more (7 v 0 p=0.011, 8 v 1 p=0.028, 9 v 2 p=0.046); NOT WORSE at F(S) <= 3 and F(S) <= F(K) + 2 (3 of 36 upper
  bound 20.2%; 0 of 36 8.0%); anything else NOT SETTLED and the maintainer decides.
- **KEEP** (a stronger word, used only with NOT WORSE): the bullet is also shown active: NAMED-NONE(S) - NAMED-NONE(K) >= 6 of 24 on the
  decoy, or on the seam O(S) >= 8 of 12 and O(S) - O(K) >= 6 (8 v 2 p=0.036). NOT WORSE without that reads "harmless and not shown to
  act": the bullet stays under WSD-111 unless the maintainer cuts it for its bytes.
- **HARM**, reported and handed to the maintainer, never a cut by itself: S exceeds K by 4 or more runs whose last `dotnet test` summary is
  FAILED (this host's Application Control blocked `dotnet test` in 8 of 12 B-528 runs, so it may be not examined).
- Reported, not readings: F per prompt and per arm; the one-sided 95% upper bound on F(S) at the final n; cost by medians; the seam's
  O per arm.
